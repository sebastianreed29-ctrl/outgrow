Help

Questions about Outgrow? Write to outgrowhq1@gmail.com. We usually reply within a couple of days.

Finding it hard right now? Free help to quit nicotine:

  • Denmark: Stoplinien, 80 31 31 31, stoplinien.dk.
  • Sweden: Sluta-röka-linjen, 020-84 00 00, slutarokalinjen.se (also covers snus).
  • Norway: there is no national phone line. Go to helsenorge.no and your local frisklivssentral.

In an emergency, call 112 (all three countries).

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Outgrow → Cancel Subscription. You can also go to Settings → Manage subscription inside the app. You keep access until the end of the period. If you cancel at least 24 hours before a free trial ends, you will not be charged.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Please also write to us if something did not work.

I have a new phone

Tap Restore purchases in the app with the same Apple account. What you entered is only on your old phone. It only comes with you if you move your phone using an iCloud backup or a direct transfer.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings → Delete all data. You can also simply delete the app. We have no copy of what you write. If you want your purchase information deleted at our subscription provider, write to us with the ID the app shows under Settings.

Is the app free?

SOS and free help are always free. The rest requires a subscription or a lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.
