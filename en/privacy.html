Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

In short

  • Everything you write in Outgrow is stored only on your phone. That includes, for example, your name, your use, your cravings, your check-ins, your reasons and your letter to yourself.
  • We have no server, you have no account, and we cannot see your data.
  • No ads, no tracking and no selling of data. Anonymous statistics only if you say yes.
  • The only things that leave your phone are what is needed to manage a subscription. This is explained below.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Reed Digital, Danmark. If you have any questions, write to outgrowhq1@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

When you use the app, it saves what you enter locally on your phone. Some of it may say something about your use of nicotine and may therefore be health data. We never receive it, it is not sent to anyone else, and we do not use it for anything. Because we have no access to it, we do not process it as a data controller.

If you have turned on backup in iOS, for example iCloud backup, your phone may include your app data in its backup. That is controlled by Apple and your own settings, not by us.

Widgets: If you add a widget to your Home or Lock Screen, the app saves a small summary (for example time without pouches, money saved, days in a row and your colour) in a shared space on your phone so the widget can show it. It never leaves your phone.

You delete everything with Settings → Delete all data in the app, or by deleting the app.

4. What the app does not do

  • No account, no login and no email.
  • No ads and no tracking across apps or websites. We do not ask for your advertising ID.
  • No statistics about how you use the app unless you said yes to anonymous statistics. It is off until you choose it, and you can turn it off again in Settings.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone other than those listed in section 3.
  • No automated decisions or profiling.

5. Reminders, sharing and ratings

  • Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. They do not go through any server. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS settings.
  • Sharing only happens when you tap share yourself. The image is sent through the iOS share menu to the app you choose. We do not receive it.
  • Ratings in the App Store are handled by Apple.
  • Links to, for example, Stoplinien open other websites, which have their own privacy policies.

6. Children and young people

The app may be used by people under 18. We do not collect more information about young people than about others, and as described above there is no account and no profile. Purchases are made through the Apple account, where parents can use Family Sharing and "Ask to Buy". If you are under 18, please talk to an adult before you buy anything.

7. How long we keep anything

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it or the app.
  • Anonymous statistics at PostHog: at most 12 months. If you say no, or turn it off, nothing more is sent.
  • Purchase information at RevenueCat: for as long as it is necessary to handle your purchase and to comply with bookkeeping rules. After that it is deleted. You can ask for it to be deleted sooner.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and the right to object. Write to outgrowhq1@gmail.com. Because the ID is anonymous, we can only find your purchase information if you send us the ID the app shows under Settings, or your receipt from Apple.

You can complain to Datatilsynet in Denmark (datatilsynet.dk), Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten in Sweden (imy.se) or Datatilsynet in Norway (datatilsynet.no).

9. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record your IP address for security reasons when you visit it. See GitHub's privacy statement. We use no cookies and no statistics on this site.

10. Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
